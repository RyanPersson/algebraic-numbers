    Why it’s a polyrhythm: round a rational point r/s, the quadratic dots lie exactly on 1/|z − r/s|² = s²·n/F, where n is the leading coefficient and F = s²·p(r/s) the value at the centre, and n only takes every s-th value. So if 1/r² grows steadily, one cycle being s³, the ring meets the dots of value F exactly F times per cycle and plays them at F × the base pitch, like a line sliding across a polyrhythm. Untick “Slow down” and the ring keeps a steady speed instead, so the rhythm speeds up as it closes in. Round a complex quadratic centre such as i the families are p + n·(x² + 1), evenly spaced with clock 1/r (approximately), and their values |p(i)| = √(b² + c²) are the same square roots that set the overtones of a square drumhead, √(j² + k²); round ω = eiπ/3 they are √(a² + ab + b²), as for a triangular drum.     The algebraic numbers

    Every dot is an algebraic number: a root of a polynomial with integer coefficients. Each one is drawn once, using its minimal polynomial (irreducible, coefficients with no common factor, ).

    Colour shows the leading coefficient (red = 1: the algebraic integers), or the degree . Size shrinks with the length , so fewer terms and smaller coefficients make bigger dots.

    The sizes fall off like , the Dirichlet exponent for approximating a point of the plane by algebraic numbers of degree ( on the real line, on the unit circle). That keeps the density of dots the same at every scale, so you can keep zooming: each view is computed from scratch, by finding every integer polynomial with a root there (lattice reduction + enumeration).

    Click a dot to see its minimal polynomial and the ideal it determines, with the maximal ideals above it. The panel also finds the arc of dots it lies on when you zoom in: a family , whose roots lie on a circle through the simpler number with minimal polynomial .

    Controls

    • Drag to pan, scroll or pinch to zoom, double-click to zoom in.
    • Keys: + − zoom, arrows pan, / search, Esc close.
    • V hides all the controls, leaving just the picture. Press it again to bring them back.
    • L plays or stops the sound (the speaker button opens its settings).
    • The flight button (next to settings) records a smooth zoom through views and makes a link that plays it like a video.
    • The address bar keeps the current view, so you can share a link.
